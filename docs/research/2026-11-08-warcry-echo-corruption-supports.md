# Echoing and Corrupting Cry Support Review

Reviewed 2026-11-08 against isolated PoB 0.23.1 and the PoE2 0.5.5f graph.
Three positive-tier support choices add fourteen curated pairs to the five
existing base-use Warcry utility presets. No new preferences, passive scopes,
solver behavior, schema or shared UI controls.

## Decisions

| Support | Pairings | Boundary |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Echoing Cry | 4 | Infernal, Seismic, Fortifying and standalone Ferocious; Ancestral's existing HasUsageCondition excludes it |
| Corrupting Cry I/II | 10 | All five parents, granting separate player-owned Corrupted Cry components, not parent hit or minion damage |

Native compatibility checks agree with these fourteen parent pairs. All eighteen
additional predicates for Combust, Volcanic Steps/Eruption, Shield Wave and the
two Corrupted Cry children reject these Warcry-only supports. Twenty negative
controls remove Warcry or add an existing use condition. Five positive type
controls add both Echoing's own usage condition and its marker: the expression
does not incorrectly reject its own added type. This marker control is not a
way to remove Ancestral's actual Glory gate in a live build.

Existing form/equipment requirements remain. Ferocious means standalone Bear
with Talisman, not socketed human-form replacement. Ancestral still needs Glory.
Fortifying's known native Armoured Shield validation gap remains qualified.
Compatibility is not a complete gear, sockets or linked-loadout validator.

## Echoes and Costs

Source constants specify two echoes separated by 1300 ms, -50% initial area,
+100% area per repeat, -50% counted monster Power, -40% damage and movement
distance 100 in the descriptor's decimetres, i.e. 10 metres. Use resets the
movement condition. No assumption is made about all echoes hitting, reaching
new targets, refreshing empowerment, generating Rage or applying extra stacks.

In this tested PoB copy, the constants have no calculation mapping: accepted
Echoing links add the skill types and Mana-cost multiplier, but parent radius,
damage, Power, action time, cooldown and calculated use rate remain unchanged.
The runtime does not enforce travel distance or simulate the repeat schedule.
Unchanged native damage is not evidence that the source damage penalty is absent
in game. No "three times DPS" or guaranteed stack-gain multiplier is used.

The initial support multiplier is 1.50. Native/independent isolated cost checks:
`floor(85 * 1.50) = 127` Mana, and with Efficiency II,
`ceil(floor(85 * 1.50) * .60) = 77`. Corrupting adds no multiplier, so the
combined parent costs match those amounts. Ancestral rejects Echoing and retains
its baseline 44 Mana. These are parent-use checks, not total repeat costs,
resource sustain or a free cooldown bypass.

## Corrupted Blood Ownership and Stacks

Corrupting grants `TriggeredCorruptingCryPlayer` or
`TriggeredCorruptingCryTwoPlayer` through the gem's additional effect ID. Both
are player-owned triggered Physical damage over time, not Attack, Spell or
Warcry-type hits. The parent can lack a hit entirely. A native flag explicitly
allows Warcry damage modifiers to affect the child; that is not permission to
borrow Attack, Spell or minion damage, or to score Bleeding as Corrupted Blood.

Both children inherit parent skill level 20 despite support level 1. The source
level-20 damage-per-minute value is 17217, so independent one-stack rate is
`17217 / 60 = 286.95`. Configured 1/2/10/11/20 stacks give
286.95/573.90/2869.50/2869.50/2869.50 native CorruptingBloodDPS. The native
multiplier caps effective stacks at ten even when the config value is larger.
The untouched native configuration defaults to ten stacks; this is an assumed
state, not ten applications generated by one use or demonstrated sustained DPS.
Source application is one stack per trigger. Reapplication, independent expiry,
enemy movement, supply and Echo timing are not simulated by this audit.

At one stack, +100% Physical, damage-over-time or Warcry damage independently
doubles the child rate. +100% Attack, Spell, Minion or Bleeding damage leaves it
unchanged. No minion actor is present. The parent retains its baseline hit DPS
and does not automatically include the separately selected child's damage.
Selecting Echoing alongside the child does not make Echoing support the child;
the child's type predicate still rejects it, and native one-stack DPS is
unchanged. This is not a complete Echo-trigger interaction model.

Both children have five-second source duration. Native tick arithmetic gives
`ceil(5 / .033) * .033 = 5.016` seconds. +20% generic duration gives 6.006;
Prolonged Duration I gives 6.501, without changing damage per second. These
child-duration checks do not add Prolonged to Seismic/Ferocious's base-use
support allowlists or promise sustainable stacks.

Corrupting I gives 30% less Warcry area, natively checked independently as
`floor(baseRadius * sqrt(.70))`: 40 -> 33, and Ferocious 70 -> 58. Tier II has
no Tier-I area penalty, not an assumed higher per-stack damage coefficient.
The triggered child has zero base Mana cost and no Life cost in these fixtures;
the parent still pays its normal use cost. Paquate's Pact is a separate tier-zero
mechanic and its Life-cost rules are not imported into I/II.

## Site Behavior

All three choices are context-only: no automatic DoT/Physical/Duration priority,
support multiplier or repeated reward enters routing. Existing manual weights,
defence, goals, budgets and empty parent hit scopes remain intact. Corrupting
tiers replace within one family; saved builds and JSON transfer retain allowed
choices. Seismic/Ferocious's base-duration cautions now explicitly distinguish
support-granted timers, without deleting Ancestral's legitimate embodiment
duration suggestion. Existing Exposing caution remains specific to Exposure.
